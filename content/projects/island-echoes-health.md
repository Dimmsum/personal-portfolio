Island Echoes Health connects patients with clinicians. It's a freelance build, with a web app and a mobile app sharing one backend.

## How it's built

- **API:** Express and TypeScript. All business logic lives here.
- **Web:** Next.js App Router.
- **Mobile:** Expo and React Native.
- **Data and auth:** Supabase (PostgreSQL). Every request is checked by the API, with row-level security on every table as a second layer.
